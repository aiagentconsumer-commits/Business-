Trend analysis: Momentum is durable, but execution risks are shifting

FY2026 SAMPLE DATA
Growth Momentum
Enterprise Dominance
The shift to Enterprise ($1M+ deals) is providing more predictable revenue but increasing the concentration risk. Q3 saw three major $2M+ closures.
Expansion Velocity
Net Retention Rate (NRR) is trending upward (118% in NAMER). Upselling core users into the Enterprise Suite is the most efficient growth lever.
Operational Risks
Late-Stage Slippage
15% of deals forecast for Q3 slipped to Q4 due to procurement delays. The "standard" legal review period has extended from 14 to 22 days.
Talent Bottlenecks
APAC underperformance is highly correlated with vacant Sales Engineer roles. Ramp time for new hires in EMEA is currently exceeding plan by 20%.
Market Shifts
Competitive Pricing
Competitors are aggressive in the mid-market segment. While our win rates are stable, discounting pressure is mounting to secure multi-year terms.
ROI Scrutiny
CFO-level sign-off is now required for 85% of all new business deals. Sales enablement must pivot toward "Economic Value" discovery.
