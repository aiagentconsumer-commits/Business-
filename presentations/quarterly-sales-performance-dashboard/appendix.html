Metric definitions & data notes: Transitioning to production

FY2026 SAMPLE DATA
Win Rate
Calculated as (Total Won / Total Closed) by deal count within the reporting period. Excludes deals tagged as "Disqualified" or "Merged."
Pipeline Coverage
Ratio of total weighted pipeline value to the remaining quarterly sales target.
Net Retention Rate (NRR)
The percentage of recurring revenue retained from existing customers, including expansion and minus churn/downgrades.
Qualified Lead (SQL)
A lead meeting BANT criteria (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) and accepted by Sales.
